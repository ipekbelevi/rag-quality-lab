# Returns and Refunds

## Return window

You can return items within 21 days of delivery.

- Machines: must be returned in their original packaging with all accessories.
- Pods: only unopened boxes can be returned. Opened pod boxes cannot be returned unless the pods are defective.
- Pod Club subscription boxes cannot be returned. You can skip or pause future deliveries instead.

## How to start a return

Start a return from "My Orders" > "Return an item". You will receive a KuşKargo return code by email. Drop the parcel at any KuşKargo branch within 5 days of receiving the code.

## Return shipping costs

- Defective or wrong item: return shipping is free.
- Change of mind: ₺79 return shipping is deducted from your refund.

## Refunds

After the returned item arrives at our İzmir warehouse, we inspect it within 3 business days. Approved refunds are sent within 7 business days after inspection, to the original payment method. For orders paid by bank transfer, we need your IBAN to send the refund.

Your bank may need extra time to show the refund on your card statement.

## Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges. Please return the item and place a new order.
