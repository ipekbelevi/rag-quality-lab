# Bean Points Loyalty Program

Bean Points is Belevico's free loyalty program. Every customer with a Belevico account is automatically a member.

## Earning points

- You earn 1 Bean Point for every ₺10 you spend.
- Shipping fees do not earn points.
- Pod Club members earn double points (2 points for every ₺10).
- Referral: when a friend places their first order with your referral link, you and your friend each get 250 Bean Points.
- Writing a product review earns 20 Bean Points, maximum 5 reviews per month.

## Using points

Every 100 Bean Points can be redeemed for a ₺25 discount at checkout. You can use up to 1,000 points (₺250) on a single order. Points cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Expiry

Points expire if your account has no purchases for 12 months in a row. Making any purchase resets the 12-month period.

## Returns and points

If you return an item, the points earned on that item are removed from your balance.
