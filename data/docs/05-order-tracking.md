# Tracking Your Order

## How to track an order

You can track an order in three ways:
1. In "My Orders" in your Belevico account
2. Through the tracking link in the shipping email (sent when the order status becomes Shipped)
3. By asking our virtual assistant or live chat, using your order number (format BLV- followed by 6 digits)

## What order statuses mean

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Received | We received your order and payment. |
| Preparing | Your order is being packed at our İzmir warehouse. Orders usually stay in this status for up to 1 business day. |
| Shipped | The parcel was handed to KuşKargo. A tracking link is emailed to you. |
| Out for delivery | The courier will deliver the parcel today. |
| Delivered | The parcel was delivered. |
| Delayed | There is a delay at the carrier or warehouse. We email you a new estimated date. |
| Cancelled | The order was cancelled and any payment is being refunded. |

## My order is delayed

If your order shows Delayed for more than 3 business days, contact live chat. If a Standard delivery arrives more than 5 business days late, you receive 100 Bean Points as an apology.
