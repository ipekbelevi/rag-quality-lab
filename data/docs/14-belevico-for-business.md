# Belevico for Business

Belevico for Business is our program for offices, cafés and hotels.

## Who can join

Companies that order 10 or more machines, or subscribe to at least 3 Office plans, can join Belevico for Business. To apply, email business@belevico.example with your company name, tax number and the number of employees.

## Benefits

- A dedicated account manager
- 15% discount on machines
- Invoicing with payment terms of net 30 days
- Priority repair: business machines are repaired within 5 business days instead of 10
- Free on-site setup in İstanbul, Ankara and İzmir

## Office plan reminder

The Office plan is a Pod Club plan with 12 boxes (120 pods) per month for ₺1,790. Business customers can combine several Office plans under one invoice.
