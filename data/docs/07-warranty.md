# Warranty

## Warranty periods

| Machine | Standard warranty |
|---------|-------------------|
| Belevico Aria | 2 years |
| Belevico Forte | 3 years |
| Belevico Nomad | 1 year |

The warranty starts on the delivery date.

## Belevico Care+

If you register your machine in the Belevico Home app within 30 days of delivery, you get Belevico Care+: one extra year of warranty, free of charge. Machines registered after 30 days do not qualify for Care+.

## What the warranty covers

The warranty covers manufacturing defects in parts and workmanship.

The warranty does not cover:
- Damage caused by limescale because the machine was not descaled
- Damage caused by using third-party pods
- Damage caused by descaling with vinegar or non-Belevico descaling products
- Drops, liquid spills on the electronics, and other accidental damage
- Normal wear of seals and the drip tray

## How warranty repair works

Contact support with your order number and a short video of the problem. If the issue is covered, we send you a free KuşKargo pickup code. Repairs take about 10 business days at our İzmir repair center.

Belevico Forte owners receive a free loaner machine during the repair. Loaner machines are not available for Aria or Nomad.
