# About Belevico

Belevico is a coffee technology company founded in 2019 in İzmir, Turkey. We design smart pod coffee machines and produce our own compostable coffee pods, called Belevico Pods.

## Our mission

Our mission is to make café-quality coffee at home with zero plastic waste. Every Belevico Pod is made from plant-based material and is certified home-compostable within 12 weeks.

## Where we operate

Belevico currently sells and delivers only within Turkey. Our main warehouse and repair center are in İzmir (Kemalpaşa). Our showroom is in İzmir Alsancak and is open Monday to Saturday, 10:00–19:00. The showroom is closed on Sundays.
