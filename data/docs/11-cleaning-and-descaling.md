# Cleaning and Descaling

## Daily cleaning

- Empty and rinse the drip tray and used pod container every day.
- Belevico Pods are compostable, so used pods can go into a home compost bin or organic waste.

## Cleaning the LatteCore milk system (Forte only)

LatteCore rinses itself automatically after each milk drink. Once a week, remove the milk carafe and wash all its parts in warm water. The carafe parts are dishwasher-safe on the top rack.

## Descaling

Descale your machine every 3 months, or earlier when the orange drop icon lights up. A blinking red light means descaling is overdue.

Use only Belevico DescalePro (₺159, enough for 2 descaling cycles). Never use vinegar or other descalers: they damage the seals and void your warranty.

### How to descale

1. Empty the drip tray and the used pod container.
2. Mix one sachet of DescalePro with 0.5 liters of water in the water tank.
3. Hold the espresso and lungo buttons together for 3 seconds. The light blinks orange.
4. The descaling program runs automatically and takes about 25 minutes.
5. Rinse the tank, fill it with fresh water, and run 2 rinse cycles.

Damage caused by limescale because a machine was not descaled is not covered by the warranty.
