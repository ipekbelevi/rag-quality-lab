# Shipping and Delivery

## Where we deliver

We deliver to all 81 provinces of Turkey. We do not ship internationally. We cannot deliver to PO boxes.

## Delivery options and costs

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
|--------|---------------|------|
| Standard | 2–4 business days | ₺59, free for orders over ₺750 |
| Express | Next business day | ₺149 |
| Showroom pickup | Ready in 1 business day | Free |

Express delivery is only available in İstanbul, Ankara and İzmir, and only for orders placed before 14:00 on a business day. Orders placed after 14:00 count as the next business day.

Pod Club subscription deliveries always ship free with Standard delivery.

Showroom pickup is only available at our İzmir Alsancak showroom. Please bring your order number and an ID card. Unclaimed pickup orders are cancelled and refunded after 10 days.

## Delivery attempts

Our carrier, KuşKargo, makes 2 delivery attempts. If both fail, the parcel waits at the nearest KuşKargo branch for 5 days. If it is not collected within 5 days, it is returned to our warehouse and the order is refunded, minus the original shipping fee.

## Damaged parcels

If your parcel arrives visibly damaged, ask the courier to write a damage report (hasar tespit tutanağı) before you accept it, and contact us within 48 hours with photos.
