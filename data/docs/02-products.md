# Belevico Machines and Pods

## Belevico Aria

Belevico Aria is our compact everyday machine.
- Water tank: 1.1 liters
- Pump pressure: 15 bar
- Drinks: espresso, lungo, americano
- No milk system
- Price: ₺7,490

## Belevico Forte

Belevico Forte is our premium machine with a built-in milk system called LatteCore.
- Water tank: 1.8 liters
- Pump pressure: 19 bar
- Drinks: espresso, lungo, americano, cappuccino, latte, flat white
- LatteCore milk system with automatic rinse
- Price: ₺12,990

## Belevico Nomad

Belevico Nomad is a portable machine for travel and camping.
- Charges over USB-C; one full charge makes about 6 espressos
- Water tank: 0.25 liters
- Drinks: espresso only
- Price: ₺3,290

## Belevico Pods

Belevico Pods are sold in boxes of 10 pods. A box costs ₺189. We currently offer 14 blends, from the mild "Ege Sabahı" (intensity 4) to the strong "Gece Vardiyası" (intensity 12).

Belevico Pods only work with Belevico machines. Belevico machines only accept Belevico Pods; third-party pods are not supported and will trigger error E-23.
