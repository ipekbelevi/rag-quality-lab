# Setting Up Your Machine

## First use

1. Remove all packaging and tape.
2. Rinse the water tank and fill it with fresh, cold water.
3. Plug in the machine and press the power button. The machine heats up in about 25 seconds.
4. Run a rinse cycle: without a pod, press the lungo button twice. Repeat 3 times and discard the water.

## Connecting to the Belevico Home app

The Belevico Home app is available for iOS and Android.

1. Create an account or sign in to the app.
2. Hold the power button for 5 seconds until the light blinks blue.
3. In the app, tap "Add machine" and select your Wi-Fi network.

Belevico machines only connect to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks. 5 GHz networks are not supported.

Registering your machine in the app within 30 days of delivery activates Belevico Care+ (one extra year of warranty).

## Belevico Nomad setup

Belevico Nomad has no Wi-Fi and does not connect to the app. Charge it fully over USB-C before first use (about 2 hours). To register Nomad for Care+, enter its serial number manually in the app.

## Adjusting cup size

You can set the espresso volume between 25 ml and 60 ml, and the lungo volume between 80 ml and 150 ml, in the app under "Machine settings".
