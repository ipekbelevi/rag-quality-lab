# Ordering and Payment

## How to place an order

You can order on belevico.example or in the Belevico Home app. You need a Belevico account to place an order; guest checkout is not available.

After you place an order, you receive a confirmation email with your order number. Belevico order numbers always have the format BLV- followed by 6 digits, for example BLV-482913.

## Changing or cancelling an order

You can edit or cancel an order yourself within 30 minutes of placing it, from "My Orders" in your account. After 30 minutes the order goes to our warehouse and can no longer be changed. If the order has not shipped yet, live chat support may still be able to cancel it.

## Accepted payment methods

- Credit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard and Troy
- Bank transfer (EFT/havale): your order is held for 48 hours. If payment does not arrive within 48 hours, the order is cancelled automatically.

Cash on delivery is not available.

## Installments

Installments of up to 9 months are available only for Belevico Forte, and only with credit cards from partner banks shown at checkout. Pods and accessories cannot be paid in installments.

## Invoices

An e-Archive invoice is emailed to you when your order ships. Corporate invoices can be requested at checkout by entering a tax number.
