# Account and Privacy

## Password and login

- Password reset links are sent by email and are valid for 1 hour.
- You can turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) by SMS under Settings > Security.
- After 5 failed login attempts, your account is locked for 15 minutes.

## Changing your email or phone number

You can change your email address and phone number under Settings > Profile. A verification code is sent to the new email or phone number.

## Your data

Belevico processes personal data in line with KVKK (the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law). Customer data is stored on servers located in Turkey.

We use your order history to recommend blends. You can turn off personalized recommendations under Settings > Privacy.

## Marketing messages

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails with the link at the bottom of any email, or turn off SMS and email marketing under Settings > Notifications. Order and delivery messages cannot be turned off.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under Settings > Privacy > Delete Account. Account deletion is completed within 30 days. Deleting your account permanently removes your Bean Points balance and cancels any active Pod Club subscription.

Invoices are kept for 10 years because of Turkish tax law, even after account deletion.
