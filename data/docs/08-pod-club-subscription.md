# Pod Club Subscription

Pod Club is our monthly pod subscription.

## Plans

| Plan | Pods per month | Monthly price |
|------|----------------|---------------|
| Solo | 2 boxes (20 pods) | ₺349 |
| Duo | 4 boxes (40 pods) | ₺649 |
| Office | 12 boxes (120 pods) | ₺1,790 |

All plans include free Standard delivery. You can choose your blends and change them any time before the next billing date.

## Billing and shipping dates

Subscriptions are billed on the 1st of every month and ship by the 5th of the month.

## Skip, pause or cancel

- Skip: you can skip a single delivery up to 3 days before the billing date.
- Pause: you can pause your subscription for up to 3 months at a time.
- Cancel: you can cancel any time with no cancellation fee. There is no minimum subscription period. If you cancel after the billing date, that month's box still ships.

You can manage your subscription in the Belevico Home app under "Pod Club".

## Member benefits

Pod Club members earn double Bean Points on all purchases and get early access to limited-edition blends.
